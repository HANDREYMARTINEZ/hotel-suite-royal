The hotel

Warm rest,
heartfelt service

On a quiet street in Melgar, minutes from the town centre, Suite Royal brings together everything you want from Colombia’s warm lowlands: cool water, shade, good food and a team that welcomes you like family.

Bright rooms with balconies and air conditioning, a courtyard pool framed by greenery and a second rooftop pool with a bar and open views of the Tolima mountains. Both pools stay open until 11 pm.
